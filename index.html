FAQ

Questions people ask before signing up.

Does DataDost store my documents?
No. Documents are read in memory and immediately discarded. Only extracted numbers are saved. PAN and Aadhaar never stored or shown back.
Do I need to link my bank account?
Never. DataDost works entirely from documents you upload. No bank credentials, no account linking — ever. Permanent design decision.
What languages does DataDost support?
Ask in English, Hindi, Hinglish, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi — or any mix.
I'm based outside India. Does it work?
Yes. DataDost supports AED, USD, GBP, SGD, AUD and more. It detects your document's currency automatically — no settings needed.
Is DataDost a SEBI-registered advisor?
No — DataDost is a financial clarity tool, not an investment advisor. It helps you understand your own numbers so you can have better conversations with registered advisors.
When do paid plans start?
Paid subscriptions open September 2026. Your free account carries over automatically — no need to sign up again.
